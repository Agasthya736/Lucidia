Lucidia Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

Lucidia ("the app", "we") is a mobile app that analyses CT scan images you upload and produces an AI-generated draft report. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Lucidia Android app and its backend service.

Medical disclaimer. Lucidia is a research and decision-support tool. Its output is not a medical diagnosis and is not a substitute for review by a qualified radiologist or physician. Do not delay or change medical care based on the app's output.

Who we are

The app is developed by Agasthya, Madurai, India. Contact: lucidia4582@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • CT scan images that you choose to upload for analysis.
  • Generated reports: the AI-produced findings, differential diagnosis text, and PDF report created from your scan.
  • Account information email address, name or username, and a securely hashed password.
  • Technical and audit data: request timestamps and an integrity log (hash-chained records) that records which processing steps ran on each report.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no ads.

How we use it

  • To analyse the uploaded scan and generate, verify, and display a report.
  • To let you view and download your past reports.
  • To keep an audit trail that makes reports traceable, and to keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Google Cloud: our backend runs on Google Cloud Run and stores data in Google Cloud SQL (region: Mumbai, India).

Third-Party AI Processing (Google Gemini API)

To provide automated visual and clinical analysis, images you submit through Lucidia are transmitted securely to Google LLC’s Gemini API.

Because Lucidia uses Google’s paid commercial API tier:

  • Your submitted images, prompts, and analysis results are not used by Google to train or improve its AI models.
  • Data transmitted to Google is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.
  • Data is processed to generate the requested analysis report. Under Google’s paid-tier terms, Google may keep prompts and responses for a limited period solely to detect abuse and to meet legal obligations.

We do not share your data with other third parties except where required by law.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Stored data is held in managed cloud databases with access restricted to the service. No system is perfectly secure, so please avoid uploading images that contain your name or other identifying details burned into the pixels.

Retention and deletion

We keep your uploaded scans and reports until you delete them or ask us to. To request deletion of your data or account, use the in-app delete option or email lucidia4582@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We will act on requests within 30 days. Backups may take a short additional time to clear.

Your choices and rights

You can choose not to upload any image. You can ask us to access, correct, export, or delete the data we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

Lucidia is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify users in the app.
