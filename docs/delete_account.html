Delete Your Lucidia Account and Data

Lucidia is developed by Agasthya, based in Madurai, India. This page explains how to request deletion of your Lucidia account and associated information.

How to request deletion

You can request account deletion using either method:

  1. Through the app: Open Lucidia and use the account-deletion option, where available.
  2. By email: Email lucidia4582@gmail.com with the subject line Lucidia account deletion request. Where possible, send the request from the email address associated with your Lucidia account.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account before processing your request. We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days, subject to identity verification and applicable legal requirements. If a request requires additional time, we will communicate as appropriate.

What will be deleted

When account deletion is completed, the process is designed to remove the following information associated with your account:

  • Your account record and associated account details.
  • Scan records and uploaded medical images, including supported CT scans, X-rays, skin images, and injury images.
  • Generated report content and associated scan information stored by Lucidia.
  • Associated feedback and consent records.

Uploaded image files stored in cloud storage are also targeted for deletion as part of the account-deletion process.

Information that may be retained

  • Audit records: Certain integrity and security records may be retained in anonymised form for traceability, security, or legal purposes.
  • Backups: Residual copies may remain temporarily in backups or recovery systems until they are cleared through the applicable retention cycle.
  • Legal requirements: Information may be retained where required by applicable law or a valid legal obligation.
  • Third-party services: Information already sent to an external service provider may be subject to that provider's applicable retention and deletion procedures.

Account deletion removes information from Lucidia's active account systems as provided by the deletion process. It may not immediately erase every backup, anonymised audit record, or copy held under a third-party provider's applicable retention rules.

Deleting individual scans or reports

If you want to request deletion of particular scans or reports rather than your entire account, email lucidia4582@gmail.com with the subject Lucidia data deletion request. Identify the relevant items without including unnecessary medical information in the email.

Contact

For account deletion or questions about your data, contact: lucidia4582@gmail.com.

Read the Lucidia Privacy Policy for additional information about the information we process, storage, retention, and your privacy choices.
