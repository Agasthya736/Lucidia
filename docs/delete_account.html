Delete your Lucidia account and data

You can ask us to permanently delete your Lucidia account and the data linked to it. Lucidia is developed by Agasthya, Madurai, India.

How to request deletion

You can use either option below.

  1. In the app: use the delete option in the Lucidia app.
  2. By email: send an email to lucidia4582@gmail.com with the subject Lucidia account deletion request. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Lucidia, so we can verify the account is yours.

We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account details (name, email address, user ID).
  • CT scan images you uploaded.
  • Reports and PDFs generated from your scans.

What may be kept

  • Encrypted backups are cleared on their normal cycle, which may take a short additional time.
  • Anonymous integrity-log entries that contain no personal data, and anything we are legally required to keep.

If you only want to delete some reports rather than your whole account, say so in your email.
